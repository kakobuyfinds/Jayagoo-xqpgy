What is the idea behind a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

In this guide, it means a structured way to research fashion listings: keep product links, sizes, measurements, listed prices, and open questions together. A useful sheet helps you compare information; it does not verify a seller, guarantee quality, or confirm authenticity.

Jayagoo spreadsheet guide

Four steps. Better notes.

A short, repeatable process makes a product list easier to use than a collection of disconnected screenshots.

01

Choose a category

Start with shoes, a clothing layer, headwear, or an accessory. Define the type of item and your intended use before comparing listings.

02

Save the exact listing

Keep the source URL, product name, selected variant, currency, and date checked. Separate seller statements from your own observations.

03

Check fit and evidence

Compare measurements with an item you own. Review available close-ups and write down missing details instead of filling gaps with assumptions.

04

Review the full cost

Keep the item price separate from shipping, applicable fees, and taxes. Recheck current availability and terms before making a purchase.

Direct answers

Before you browse.

A practical starting point for readers building their first product research sheet.

What does Jayagoo spreadsheet mean in this guide?

Here, Jayagoo spreadsheet describes a product research approach: organize fashion listing links, measurements, listed prices, and comparison notes in one place. This page is an independent guide, not an official Jayagoo service or a downloadable spreadsheet.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides category links and a practical comparison checklist. You can create your own sheet using product URL, category, variant, measurements, listed price, shipping estimate, and verification notes as column headings.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing buttons open the CAFANS homepage.

Can a spreadsheet verify product authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet only organizes information. Brand names, photographs, listing descriptions, and comparison notes do not establish authenticity, seller authorization, or product quality.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing measurements with an item that already fits. For clothing, check relevant garment dimensions; for shoes, check the seller's size chart and available foot-length or insole measurements. Do not assume that the same size label fits identically across listings.

Is the displayed product price the total cost?

Not necessarily. Record the listed item price separately from any applicable domestic shipping, service fees, international shipping, taxes, and duties. Check current terms before paying.
